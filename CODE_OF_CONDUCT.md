# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We pledge to make participation in the Zaprust community a welcoming,
harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of background or
experience level. We are here to build a fast, honest piece of software
together.

## Our standards

Examples of behaviour that contributes to a positive environment:

- Giving and gracefully accepting constructive feedback.
- Reviewing the work, never attacking the person.
- Being patient with first-time contributors, everyone wrote a first PR once.
- Keeping discussions about Zaprust in Zaprust's spaces.

Examples of unacceptable behaviour:

- Harassment, insults, or discriminatory language.
- Publishing someone else's private information (including phone numbers
  and chat contents) without their explicit permission.
- Using the project's spaces to advertise spam or automation services.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may remove comments, close issues/PRs, or ban participants
who violate this code. Reports go to the project owner (fanyzp)
through a private channel listed on the repository profile. All reports
are handled discreetly.

## Scope

This code applies in all project spaces: issues, pull requests,
discussions, and any chat or event that represents the project.
