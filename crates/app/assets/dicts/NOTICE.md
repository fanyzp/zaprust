# Bundled spell-check dictionaries

The composer spell checker (row 128) bundles two Hunspell
dictionaries, embedded unmodified from the LibreOffice
dictionaries mirror (github.com/LibreOffice/dictionaries):

- `en_US.aff` / `en_US.dic`: the en_US Hunspell dictionary
  (SCOWL-derived, version 2020.12.07), under the permissive
  license terms in `README-en_US.txt`, kept alongside.
- `id_ID.aff` / `id_ID.dic`: the Indonesian dictionary from
  the hunspell-id project, under the GNU Lesser General
  Public License v3, whose full text is in `LICENSE-id_ID`,
  kept alongside as the license requires.
